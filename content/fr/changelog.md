---
title: Changelog
---

The changelog is generated at build time from
[`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/TunGuard/tanguard-binary/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
in the [`TunGuard/tanguard-binary`](https://github.com/TunGuard/tanguard-binary)
repository.

To populate it locally, run:

```sh
python scripts/sync_changelog.py
```
